How the days work

Two days all together, then a crew day. On crew days we split into smaller groups, so everyone gets more of what they love and less waiting around — and Nicholas & Sarah never go more than three days without a day of their own, while Mom, Dad & Halmoni take one of the Lodge’s guided tours or a slow day at the Lodge. Two Lodge tours — the DMZ and Nami Island — are whole-family days. Together-days run at an easy pace: late starts, taxis door-to-door, a sit-down every hour or so, and a rest back at the lodge.

Before you go

🚌 The Lodge's guided tours

  • DMZ Tunnel Tour (Day 2, everyone): 6:40–14:30, ₩80,000 pp, not Mondays or holidays; Dora Observatory + the Third Infiltration Tunnel. The tunnel ramp is steep — ask about the monorail for Halmoni. Treat it as non-refundable.
  • All-Day City Tour (Day 3, Mom · Dad · Halmoni): ₩109,000 pp — Jogyesa, Gyeongbokgung, Insadong (free time for lunch), Namdaemun market, N Seoul Tower cable car (the observation deck is extra). Departure time confirmed at booking.
  • Korean Folk Village Tour (Day 7, Mom · Dad · Halmoni): 9:00–13:00, ₩90,000 pp. Wheelchairs can be rented at the entrance.
  • Nami Island Tour (Day 13, everyone): 8:00–17:00, ₩110,000 pp. Free wheelchairs on the island; lunch not included.
  • All tours leave from and return to Gate 1. Book at least a day ahead with the Discover Seoul desk (DiscoverSeoulDesk@dragonhilllodge.com · +82 2-6903-6286/6234). Passports; bring won (cards may not be accepted); full refund only if you cancel 24h+ ahead.

🪪 Getting on & off post

  • Dragon Hill Lodge is inside USAG Yongsan, near Gate 1. DoD ID holders show their ID plus a second ID at the gate.
  • Anyone without a DoD ID needs sponsorship — start 30+ days out. Carry passports every day.
  • Only AAFES taxis can drive onto post: call or text 1544-9080 and ask for a van for five. Regular taxis and Kakao T drop you at Gate 1, a 5-min walk from Samgakji Stn exit 13. Gate 13 (Ichon Stn exits 1–2) is a pedestrian gate about 15+ min on foot from the lodge.

🚕 Moving five people

  • Standard Seoul taxis seat 4 → use 2 cabs, or one Kakao T Venti (5–6 seats, surge-priced) or a jumbo taxi (up to 8).
  • Taxi fares on each day map are estimates per cab (≈). The base fare is ₩4,800; a 20-min ride is usually ₩10–15k.
  • Nicholas & Sarah's crew days use the subway + walking. Samgakji (Lines 4 & 6) is the nearest station.

📱 Apps to install

  • Naver Map (English mode) or KakaoMap — Google Maps can't do walking or driving directions properly in Korea.
  • Kakao T for taxis (works with foreign cards) · Papago for translation (camera mode reads menus) · Catchtable for restaurant bookings (English).
  • Every stop on the day maps has its Korean address with a copy button — paste it into Kakao T or show it to the driver.

💳 Money & power

  • The lodge is cashless (cards, Apple/Google Pay, room charge). Cards work almost everywhere; carry ₩50–100k cash for Gwangjang and Tongin markets.
  • Olive Young and most big stores do an instant tax refund with a passport on larger purchases.
  • Lodge rooms are 110V (US plugs). Everywhere else in Korea is 220V (type C/F) — phones and laptops are fine with an adapter.

🏨 At the Lodge

  • Greenstreet: breakfast 6:30–10:45, lunch 11–14, dinner 17–21:30 (Tue Tacos Buffet; Fri–Sat Prime Rib Buffet). The Deli 6:00–16:00. Sables Fri–Sat 17–21.
  • Bentley's Bar: Sun–Thu 16–23, Fri–Sat 15–01. The POiNT: gym & sauna, pool (weekdays 12–20, weekends 8–20).
  • Clippers Spa & Hair Salon in Market Square · Discover Seoul tour desk (Mon–Sat 9–18, Sun 9–17) · free washers & dryers on every floor · check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00 · front desk +82 2-790-0016.

🏯 Palaces, museums & Halmoni

  • Gyeongbokgung: free for 65+ (show passport) and free wheelchairs at the info center (ID deposit). The main axis is paved and flat; side courtyards are gravel.
  • Museums (National Museum of Korea, Leeum) have elevators throughout — ask the info desk about wheelchairs.
  • N Seoul Tower without the hill: the free Namsan Oreumi inclined elevator → cable car → ramps and elevators to the tower plaza.
  • Every together-day is built at Halmoni's pace: taxis door-to-door, a sit-down every 60–90 min, and home by mid-afternoon on long days.

🆘 If something goes wrong

  • 119 ambulance/fire · 112 police · 1330 Korea Travel Hotline (English, 24h) · lodge front desk +82 2-790-0016.
  • Pharmacies (약국) are everywhere; the Ready Young pharmacies on the maps are tourist-friendly and open late.

Why the days are in this order

MonThe DMZ tour doesn't run, and most museums, Deoksugung, Leeum, galleries, Amore and Woo Lae Oak are closed → Mondays are Starfield/Gangnam (Day 5) and the easy Seochon lunch day (Day 12).
TueGyeongbokgung and Bar Cham are closed → Tuesdays are Halmoni's Table / Deoksugung (Day 6) and Nami Island (Day 13).
WedThe history museum and the Seoul Museum of Art stay open late, and Seun Sangga is open → Sarah & Dad's museum day (Day 14).
Thu–SatAudeum only opens these days → Nicholas & Dad's crew day is a Saturday (Day 10).
SatMend Clinic is open only 9:30–13:30 on Saturdays (closed Thu & Sun) → the pamper day starts at the clinic (Day 10).
Tue–FriOnjium only serves these days → Halmoni's crew day (with the optional upgrade) is a Tuesday (Day 6).
FriJet lag gets everyone up at dawn the first mornings → the 6:40 DMZ pickup goes on Day 2.
SunGalleries and Bar Cham are open (they close Mon/Tue) → Nicholas & Sarah's palace-galleries-Bar Cham day (Day 11); Jinmi Sikdang is closed Sundays, so it's on a Tuesday.
